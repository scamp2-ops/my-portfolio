# my-portfolio
My personal portfolio website showcasing my IT skills, projects, and experience.

## Accessibility Improvements

For this project, I worked on making my website easier for everyone to use, including people with disabilities.

### Accessibility Fixes

- **Image Descriptions:** I checked my images to make sure they have accurate alt text so people using screen readers can understand them.
- **Keyboard Accessibility:** I checked that visitors can navigate the website using a keyboard and see which link is selected.
- **Responsive Layout:** I checked my pages at different screen sizes to make sure the content is readable without scrolling sideways.
- **Text Readability:** I checked that text is easy to read against the background colors.
- **Page Structure:** I reviewed my headings and page layout to make the content easier to follow.

### Accessibility Testing

I previously tested my website using WAVE and manually checked keyboard navigation, image descriptions, and mobile responsiveness. I will recheck all three pages after completing the new updates.

### Color Contrast

I used a consistent color palette throughout my website.
My main colors are dark blue, light blue, white, and
orange. I checked the contrast ratios to make sure the
text is easy to read.

The dark blue and white combination has a contrast
ratio of 8.77:1, and the dark blue text on the light
blue navigation background has a ratio of 7.03:1.
Both pass WCAG AA standards.

I also added visible keyboard focus outlines to make
navigation easier for people who do not use a mouse.

## Gestalt Design Principles

I used two Gestalt principles to make my website
more organized and easier to navigate.

### 1. Proximity

I used proximity by grouping related information
inside sections with spacing and borders. This
helps visitors understand which information
belongs together without making the pages look
cluttered.

### 2. Similarity

I used similarity by keeping the same colors,
headings, borders, and section styles throughout
my website. This makes all three pages look
connected and helps visitors recognize similar
types of content.
